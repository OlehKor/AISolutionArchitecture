# Parking Details

## Levels and zones
The garage has three levels and four zones:
- Zone A is on Level 0 and is meant for short stays; it is closest to the street exit.
- Zone B is on Level -1 and is the standard zone for stays of several hours.
- Zone C is on Level -2 and is the long-stay zone for full-day and multi-day parking.
- Zone EV is on Level -1 near the east lift and is equipped with electric vehicle chargers.
Current prices and free space counts for each zone are provided by the live data of the garage.

## Vehicle size limits
The maximum vehicle height is 2.1 metres, the maximum width is 2.0 metres and the maximum length is 5.3 metres. The maximum weight is 2.5 tonnes. Vans, minibuses and roof-box equipped vehicles exceeding these limits cannot enter.

## Electric vehicle charging
Zone EV offers 20 spaces, each with a 22 kW AC Type 2 charger. Bring your own Type 2 cable. Charging is billed by energy used, in addition to the zone's parking price. Charging sessions are started with the garage mobile app or a contactless card at the charger.

## Accessible parking
There are 10 accessible spaces on Level 0 next to the lifts. They are wider than standard spaces and are free of charge for holders of a valid disability parking badge. Accessible spaces cannot be reserved in advance.

## Security and safety
The garage is monitored by CCTV 24/7 and patrolled by security staff. Lighting is motion-activated, and every level has emergency exits, fire sprinklers and intercoms. Recordings are kept for 30 days.

## Motorcycles and bicycles
Motorcycles are welcome in Zone A, where marked motorcycle bays are located near the entrance. Bicycle racks are available on Level 0 free of charge.
