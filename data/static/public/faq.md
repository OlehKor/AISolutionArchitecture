# Frequently Asked Questions

## What if I lose my parking ticket?
Go to any pay station and press the help button, or call customer support. We will find your entry time from the licence plate recognition system and you pay the regular tariff for your actual stay. A lost ticket is not charged extra.

## Can I leave my car overnight?
Yes. Overnight parking is allowed in all zones and is charged by the hour up to the daily maximum. Zone C is the most economical choice for stays longer than one day.

## Is the garage open on public holidays?
Yes, the garage operates on public holidays. Holiday opening hours follow the Sunday schedule shown in the live data.

## Do you offer monthly subscriptions?
Yes, monthly subscriptions are available for Zone C. They are arranged directly with customer support and are not handled by this assistant.

## Can I get an invoice for my parking?
Yes. Invoices for companies are issued on request. Send the licence plate number, the date of parking and your company details to customer support by e-mail.

## Can I park a car with a roof box or bike rack?
Only if the total height does not exceed 2.1 metres. Measure the vehicle with the roof box before arriving, because oversized vehicles are stopped by the height sensor at the entrance.

## Are there lockers or luggage storage?
There is no luggage storage. Luggage trolleys are available at the pedestrian exit on Level 0.
