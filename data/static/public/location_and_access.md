# Location and Access

## Address
SkyPark Central is located at 12 Gediminas Avenue, 01103 Vilnius, Lithuania, directly below the Cathedral Square business district. GPS coordinates: 54.6872 N, 25.2797 E.

## How to get here by car
Arriving from the south (Kaunas highway A1), follow signs to the city centre, take Konstitucijos Avenue and then turn onto Gediminas Avenue. The vehicle entrance is on the right side of the street, 150 metres after the Lukiškės Square junction. Arriving from the north, take the Ukmergės Street exit and follow Gediminas Avenue towards the Cathedral.

## Public transport
The nearest bus stop is Katedros Aikštė (lines 1, 3 and 11), two minutes on foot from the pedestrian exit. Trolleybus line 7 stops at Lukiškės Square, five minutes away. The Vilnius railway station is 25 minutes away on foot or 10 minutes by bus line 3.

## Entrances and exits
- Vehicle entrance and exit: Gediminas Avenue 12, two lanes in, two lanes out.
- Pedestrian exit A: Gediminas Avenue, next to the main entrance.
- Pedestrian exit B: Cathedral Square, reached from Level 0 by the east lift.
- Vehicles taller than 2.0 metres must use the left lane, which has an additional height sensor.

## Walking distances to landmarks
- Vilnius Cathedral: 3 minutes on foot
- Gediminas Castle Tower: 8 minutes on foot
- Lukiškės Square: 4 minutes on foot
- Old Town and Town Hall Square: 12 minutes on foot
