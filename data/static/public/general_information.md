# General Information

## About SkyPark Central
SkyPark Central is a modern three-level underground parking garage in the centre of Vilnius, Lithuania. It has been operating since 2018 and offers short-stay, long-stay, electric-vehicle and accessible parking for private and business customers.

## Services
- Short-stay and long-stay parking, hourly or daily
- Online reservation of parking spaces through this assistant
- Electric vehicle charging stations
- Accessible parking spaces close to the lifts
- Free air pump, tyre-pressure station and vacuum cleaners on Level 0
- Luggage trolleys at the pedestrian exit on Level 0
- 24/7 CCTV monitoring

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash at the pay stations. Pay stations are located next to the lifts on every level. You can also pay at the exit barrier with a contactless card. Payment for reservations is made on exit, not in advance.

## Rules on site
- Speed limit inside the garage is 10 km/h.
- Engines must be switched off while parked.
- Storing hazardous or flammable materials in vehicles is not allowed.
- Smoking and open flames are prohibited throughout the garage.
- Parking is at the driver's own risk; the garage is not responsible for valuables left in vehicles.
